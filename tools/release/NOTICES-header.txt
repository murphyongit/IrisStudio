Not in IrisStudio.exe, and never shipped with it:
  - OpenAssetTools (https://github.com/Laupetin/OpenAssetTools, GPL-3): the launcher downloads its release v0.34.0
    from GitHub when you allow it, and checks it, to build the character zones.
  - FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org, GPL-3 in this build): when you accept the setup and the mod has none, the launcher
    downloads gyan.dev's essentials build of FFmpeg 9.0.2 from GitHub (github.com/GyanD/codexffmpeg), checks it, and
    keeps its ffmpeg.exe for capture.
  - The character zones and the animation pack: the launcher builds them on your computer from your own copy of
    Black Ops, whose files are Activision's.
